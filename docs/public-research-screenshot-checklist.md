# Public Research Screenshot Checklist

Use only the allow-listed public-research workspace before taking portfolio
screenshots. Do not include terminal windows, filesystem paths, local model
names, private sessions, or any Economist material.

1. Open `/public-research` with one public report selected. Capture the library
   and its title, filename, page count, and abbreviated SHA-256 provenance.
2. Ask: “How many common principles does the UK AI Playbook define for government
   organisations?” Capture the completed answer and expand its evidence card.
   Confirm the page-9 publisher link is visible.
3. Select the AI Safety report and UK AI Playbook. Capture a completed
   cross-document mitigation/accountability answer with evidence cards from both
   reports.
4. Open a reviewed observation. Capture its status, source/page provenance,
   revision history, and the notice that memory is advisory and fresh citations
   are required.
5. Capture an insufficient-evidence result for a question absent from the
   selected reports. Ensure it displays no fabricated citations.

Before sharing, inspect every screenshot at full size: source titles and page
numbers must be legible, citation links must resolve to public publisher URLs,
and no raw PDF passages or private data may be visible.
